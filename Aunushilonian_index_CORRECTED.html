Our Story

More Than a School Community

Aunushilonian brings together generations of Aunushilon Academy students and teachers in one connected community.

Who Are Aunushilonians?

Aunushilonians are the students and alumni of Aunushilon Academy, along with teachers who remain connected to the community. The goal is to keep the bond between generations alive and create opportunities to learn, share, collaborate and inspire one another.

Aunushilon Academy

Aunushilon Academy is located in Bheola Manik Char, Chakaria, Cox's Bazar. It was established in 1996 and is identified by EIIN 131277. The Head Teacher is Shofiqul Alam.


Aunushilonian is built around the idea that the relationship with a school does not end after graduation — it continues through community, contribution and shared memories.
